All photographs in `public/images/photos/` come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under the licences below.
Each file was downsized to 2000 px wide and re-compressed as JPEG (quality 80). No other edits were made; crops are
applied in the browser via `object-fit`/`object-position`. Credits are also shown on the site at `/credits`, and the
manifest lives in `src/lib/media.ts`.

## Known gaps

- No openly licensed photo of a passenger paying with a phone or using Mobile Money in Kigali was found on Commons.
  Payment moments are shown with the app's own UI mockups instead of a stock photo from elsewhere.
- The illustrative SVG scenes in `public/images/*.svg` (from `scripts/generate-scenes.mjs`) are no longer used by the
  landing page.
